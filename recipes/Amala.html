Amala Recipe

Ingredients and Equipment

  • Yam flour (Elubo)
  • Water
  • Pot
  • Spoon
  • wooden spatula (omorogun)

    • Step by step instructions

    1. Boil water in a pot
    2. Pour the yam flour into the boiling water
    3. Stir continuously with a wooden spatula until it thickens
    4. Continue stirring until it reaches a smooth consistency
    5. Serve hot with your choice of soup or stew
