Abacha Recipe

Description

Abacha, also known as African salad, is a traditional Nigerian dish made from dried shredded cassava.

It is a popular delicacy among the Igbo people and is often served with ugba (oil bean seeds), garden eggs, and a variety of seasonings.

Ingredients and Equipment

  • Dried shredded cassava (Abacha)
  • Ugba (oil bean seeds)
  • Garden eggs
  • Seasonings (salt, pepper, etc.)
  • Oil for frying

Step by step instructions

  1. Shred the dried cassava into small pieces.
  2. Fry the shredded cassava in oil until golden brown.
  3. Prepare the ugba and garden eggs separately.
  4. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
  5. Serve hot with your choice of soup or stew.
