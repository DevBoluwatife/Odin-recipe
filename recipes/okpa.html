Okpa Recipe

Description

Okpa is a traditional Nigerian dish made from Bambara groundnut flour.

It is a popular breakfast food in many parts of Nigeria and is often served with pap or custard.

Ingredients and Equipment

  • Bambara groundnut flour (Okpa flour)
  • luke warm water
  • Salt
  • Red Oil
  • Ground fresh red or yellow chili pepper
  • Eggs
  • Pot
  • Spoon
  • Steamer or pot with lid

Step by step instructions

  1. Mix the Bambara groundnut flour with water to form a smooth batter.
  2. Add salt and oil to the batter and mix well.
  3. Pour the batter into greased molds or banana leaves.
  4. Add egg into it
  5. Steam the okpa for about 30-40 minutes until it is firm and cooked through.
  6. Allow it to cool slightly before serving.
